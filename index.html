Foundation Year & APS Restrictions (Indian Applicants)

Under current German APS guidelines for India, Foundation Programs (Studienkolleg/Preparatory) are not eligible for APS verification for Indian Higher Secondary Certificates. Applicants from India must qualify for direct entry (CBSE/ICSE/State Board with GISMA Entrance Test) or complete 1 Year of Bachelor's degree in India before applying.

Non-Business Background Conversion Rules

Applicants holding non-business undergraduate degrees (e.g. Engineering, Arts, Humanities, Science) can qualify for GISMA Business Masters or MBA through our conversion pathways:

1. Pre-Masters Foundation Module

A 1-semester preparatory path covering fundamental economics, accounting, and business administration principles before joining the core Master's program.

2. Work Experience Waiver

If you have at least 1-2 years of full-time professional experience in a commercial, managerial, or analytical role, the academic field restriction is waived.

GISMA Internal Entrance Test & English Proficiency

Applicants without standard English certificates (IELTS/TOEFL) or needing academic qualification verification must complete the proctored GISMA Entrance Assessment.

Module 1

Password English Test

Assesses Reading, Writing, Listening, and Grammar. Equivalent to IELTS 6.0 - 6.5 standards. Fully online & proctored.

Module 2

16-Question Mathematics Test

Multiple-choice quantitative test testing high-school algebra, arithmetic, logic, and data interpretation.

Module 3

Academic Essay (300+ Words)

Timed written assessment evaluating logical argumentation, structured thought, and formal academic writing skills.

Proctoring & Retake Regulations:

Tests are webcam-monitored. A fee of €50 applies for test retakes if an applicant fails to meet the minimum threshold on the first attempt.
